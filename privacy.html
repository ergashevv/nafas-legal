English version

Nafas — ayollar bir-biri bilan tajriba almashadigan jamoa ilovasi. Bu yerda nimalarni yig‘ishimiz, ularni nima uchun ishlatishimiz va qanday o‘chirishingiz mumkinligi yozilgan.

Biz yig‘adigan ma’lumotlar

  • Hisob: Apple orqali kirganingizda Apple bergan identifikator va elektron pochta (yashirin pochta bo‘lishi mumkin).
  • Profil: ismingiz, «Men haqimda» matni, holatingiz, qiziqishlaringiz va bezak sozlamalaringiz.
  • Siz yozgan kontent: postlar, izohlar, so‘rovnomadagi ovozlar, yurakchalar, saqlanganlar.
  • Xabarlar: shaxsiy suhbatdagi matnlar, rasmlar va ovozli xabarlar.
  • Telefon raqamingiz (ixtiyoriy): «Tanishlarim meni topa olsin» ni yoqsangiz, raqamingizning o‘zi emas, undan olingan qaytarib bo‘lmaydigan kod (xesh) saqlanadi.
  • Kontaktlar (ixtiyoriy): «Kontaktlardan topish» ni bossangiz, telefoningizdagi raqamlardan kodlar telefonning o‘zida hisoblanib, faqat solishtirish uchun yuboriladi. Ismlar va raqamlarning o‘zi telefoningizdan chiqmaydi va kodlar saqlanmaydi.
  • Bildirishnoma tokeni: sizga push yuborish uchun.
  • So‘nggi faollik vaqti: «online» holatini ko‘rsatish uchun. Sozlamalarda yashirish mumkin.

Kundalik faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi va bizga yuborilmaydi.

Ismsiz yozish

Ismsiz yozgan post va izohlaringiz boshqa a’zolarga hech qachon profilingiz bilan bog‘lanib ko‘rsatilmaydi. Texnik jihatdan ular hisobingizga bog‘langan holda saqlanadi — bu qoidabuzarlikka qarshi kurashish va hisobingiz o‘chirilganda ularni ham o‘chirish uchun kerak.

Ma’lumotlardan foydalanish

  • Ilovaning o‘zi ishlashi uchun: lenta, guruhlar, xabarlar, bildirishnomalar.
  • Xavfsizlik uchun: qoidalarga zid matnlar (terrorizm, bolalarga oid jinsiy kontent, jinsiy takliflar, giyohvand moddalar, firibgarlik, spam) avtomatik tekshiriladi va o‘chiriladi; shikoyatlarni moderatorlar ko‘rib chiqadi.
  • Kimdir suhbat ustidan shikoyat qilsa, moderator faqat shikoyat qilingan odamning o‘sha suhbatdagi so‘nggi xabarlarini ko‘radi. Boshqa yozishmalar hech kimga ko‘rsatilmaydi.

Biz reklama ko‘rsatmaymiz, sizni boshqa ilova va saytlarda kuzatmaymiz va ma’lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz yoki uchinchi tomonga bermaymiz.

Saqlanish joyi

Ma’lumotlar Supabase xizmatida, Yevropa Ittifoqidagi (Frankfurt) serverlarda saqlanadi. Push bildirishnomalar Apple Push Notification xizmati orqali yuboriladi.

Hisobni o‘chirish

Ilovada Profil → Sozlamalar → Hisobni o‘chirish orqali hisobingizni istalgan vaqtda o‘chirishingiz mumkin. Profilingiz, postlaringiz, izohlaringiz, xabarlaringiz, rasm va ovozli xabarlaringiz darhol o‘chiriladi, Apple bilan kirish ulanishi bekor qilinadi. Qoidabuzarlik qayd etilgan bo‘lsa, uning qisqa yozuvi xavfsizlik maqsadida hisob bilan birga o‘chiriladi.

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

Nafas is a community app where women share experience with each other. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: the identifier and email address Apple provides when you sign in with Apple (this may be a private relay address).
  • Profile: your name, bio, status, interests and appearance settings.
  • Your content: posts, comments, poll votes, hearts and saves.
  • Messages: text, photos and voice notes in private conversations.
  • Your phone number (optional): if you turn on “Let people who know me find me”, we store a one-way hash of your number, never the number itself.
  • Contacts (optional): if you tap “Find from contacts”, hashes of the phone numbers in your address book are computed on your device and sent only to be matched. Names and numbers never leave your phone, and the hashes are not stored.
  • Push token: to deliver notifications to you.
  • Last-seen time: to show “online”. You can hide it in Settings.

Your diary is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us.

Anonymous posting

Posts and comments you write anonymously are never shown to other members together with your profile. They are stored linked to your account so that abuse can be handled and so that they are deleted with your account.

How we use data

  • To run the app: feed, groups, messages and notifications.
  • For safety: text that breaks the rules (terrorism, child sexual content, sexual solicitation, drugs, scams, spam) is checked and removed automatically, and reports are reviewed by moderators.
  • When someone reports a conversation, a moderator sees only the reported person’s recent messages in that conversation. No other messages are shown to anyone.

We show no ads, do not track you across other apps or websites, and do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Push notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, posts, comments, messages, photos and voice notes are deleted immediately and your Sign in with Apple connection is revoked.

Age

Nafas is intended for users aged 18 and over.
